Privacy notice

For the visahub.me website. Last updated 10 November 2026.

This notice explains what happens to personal data when you visit this website or write to us. We've kept it short because the website does very little with your data.

Who we are

The controller is M.Y. VISAHUB LTD, a company registered in Cyprus (no. HE 464870), with its registered office at Kyriakou Matsi 18, Victory Tower, 1st floor, 1082 Nicosia, Cyprus. You can reach us at info@visahub.me.

What this website does not do

  • It sets no cookies and uses no similar storage on your device.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It has no forms and no accounts.
  • It loads no third-party scripts. The fonts are served from our own site, not from a font service.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic technical details of your request (such as browser type and the page requested) in order to deliver the page and keep the service secure. GitHub may process this data in the United States, under its own privacy statement. We don't receive or have access to these logs.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you email us

If you write to info@visahub.me, your message is forwarded to, and read by, the company's founders. We use your email address, your name and whatever you choose to tell us only to answer you and follow up on your enquiry. Our email service providers handle the messages for us.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep enquiry emails only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up, and delete them when they're no longer needed. Please don't send us identity documents or other sensitive information by email.

Who we share data with

We don't sell personal data, and we share it only with the service providers named above (website hosting and email), or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask us for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to our use of it, and to receive it in a portable form. To use any of these rights, write to info@visahub.me. We'll answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Cyprus this is the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.cy).

What this notice doesn't cover

This notice is about the visahub.me website only. The VisaHub platform, used by recruitment agencies, has its own privacy information, which is given to the people who use it and to the people whose data it holds.

Changes

If we change this notice we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
