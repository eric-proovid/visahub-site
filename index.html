From a pile of documents to a placed worker.

VisaHub is a platform for recruitment agencies that bring overseas workers to employers in Cyprus. It reads applicants' documents, organises every candidate in one place and follows each case to placement.

The problem

Recruiting from abroad runs on paperwork.

Every applicant arrives as a bundle of scans: passport, certificates, medical and police papers, references. They come by email and messaging apps from partner agencies in several countries, in different formats and at different times.

  • Staff retype the same details into spreadsheets and forms.
  • It's hard to see which papers are missing or about to expire.
  • Each case lives in someone's inbox, so nobody sees the whole pipeline.
  • Building a shortlist for an employer means searching it all again.

How it works

Four steps, one place.

  1. Receive

    Partner agencies abroad send each applicant's documents. We start with the Philippines and India.

  2. Read and organise

    AI reads the documents, sorts them by person and fills in the candidate's record. It flags anything that needs a human check, and your staff confirm before anything is saved.

  3. Track

    Every candidate sits on one board, from application to placement. Your team moves each case forward. VisaHub flags what needs attention and never moves a case by itself.

  4. Match and present

    Compare candidates with an employer's requirements and present a matched shortlist.

Who it's for

Built for agencies. Better for everyone in the chain.

  • Recruitment agencies

    One organised candidate database and one view of every case, instead of inboxes and spreadsheets.

  • Employers

    Households, hotels and restaurants receive clear shortlists of candidates matched to what they asked for, through their agency.

  • Workers

    Their documents are handled once, with care, by agencies that can see exactly where each application stands.

We start with domestic workers and hospitality staff.

Why VisaHub

Built for sensitive documents.

Passports and personal papers deserve careful handling. These are the choices we've made so far.

  • Hosted in the EU

    Our servers run in an AWS data centre region in the European Union (Stockholm).

  • Encrypted

    Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, with an encryption key we manage.

  • Closed by default

    No public sign-up. The beta is built for invitation-only accounts with two-step sign-in and role-based access.

  • People decide

    AI prepares and flags. Agency staff check and decide.
